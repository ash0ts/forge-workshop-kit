## How to read the diagrams

Arrowheads indicate the direction of a request, response, version handoff, or record handoff; the adjacent label names which. Dashed branch paths carry execution records into a decision. They do not mean a proposal was adopted. Candidate colors distinguish alternatives, not quality. Result labels state pass, fail, or unavailable explicitly. Brackets on the KEEP frame distinguish unchanged executable content from new records.

All new diagrams are local SVG/Vue components with no timed motion. Marker IDs use Vue's `useId` where markers are needed, so simultaneously mounted slides do not share arrow definitions. Native captures remain unchanged and have their own [provenance](sources.md).
